Rehan Sanadi (India)

Founder & Club Lead · Apple Developer Club SGU

Rehan Sanadi is a computer science student, iOS engineer, and young developer from Maharashtra, India, focused on building products around Apple technologies, artificial intelligence, and real-world problem solving.

Currently pursuing a B.Tech in Computer Science with a specialization in AI & Data Science at Sanjay Ghodawat University, Rehan has spent much of his college journey building iOS applications with Swift, SwiftUI, Xcode, SwiftData, Core ML, MapKit, AVFoundation, and Apple’s on-device intelligence technologies.

Alongside academics, Rehan has worked as a Software Developer Intern at Neuroverse Software Solutions, contributing to SwiftUI interfaces, application features, debugging, and collaborative development workflows.

One of his major current projects is Suzzme, a voice-first personal assistant for iPhone and Mac. Suzzme is designed to understand a user’s daily communications, summarize important information, provide proactive reminders, and help complete actions through natural voice interaction. Rehan is building the project around Apple’s privacy-focused, on-device AI ecosystem, with a strong emphasis on minimal design, local intelligence, and a seamless Apple-style experience.

His other projects include Hearly, an AI-powered voice isolation system; Solace, a sunlight and Vitamin D tracking app; Roam, an intelligent travel companion; and ResQAssist, an emergency-response platform.

Beyond building apps, Rehan founded and leads the Apple Developer Club at Sanjay Ghodawat University, where he works to introduce students to Swift, SwiftUI, Xcode, Apple design principles, and opportunities such as the Swift Student Challenge.

For Rehan, technology is less about simply writing code and more about creating experiences that feel useful, intuitive, and human. His long-term focus is to continue building Apple-platform products that combine AI, voice interaction, privacy, and thoughtful design.
