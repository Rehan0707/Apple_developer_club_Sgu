THE CLUB SYSTEM

How it works.

A simple path from joining to building, meeting other creators, and keeping a record of the events you complete.

01

Join

Go to Join the Club and sign in. Your member portal keeps your event registrations and badges together.

02

Choose an event

Browse upcoming events. Each published event has its own details, date, location, and registration availability.

03

Register

Reserve your place for an open event. Save your QR entry pass after registering; members can reopen it from their portal.

04

Show up

Bring your QR pass to the event. The team checks your name and confirms attendance when the event starts.

05

Earn the badge

When attendance is confirmed, that event’s badge appears in your member collection. Registering alone does not unlock it.

06

Keep exploring

Use learning resources, meet other students, and join the next event whenever you are ready.

WHAT WE DO

Learn. Build. Share.

The club is a place for students to explore Apple platforms together. Events and resources can cover app ideas, interface design, Swift, SwiftUI, Xcode, projects, and community collaboration. You can start as a beginner.

Learn together

Discover tools and concepts through club sessions and curated developer resources.

Make things

Turn ideas into designs, prototypes, and apps as you build your skills.

Find community

Meet fellow students, exchange feedback, and share what you create.

Exact session topics are published on the Events page as events are announced.

THE BADGE COLLECTION

10 designs. One event at a time.

The club starts with ten event badges. Each design is assigned to a specific event by the team. The first event uses First Spark; completing that event earns it. The other designs stay locked until their events are announced and your attendance is confirmed.

EVENTS & ENTRY

Your place at the next event.

Open an event to see its latest details. Registration is separate from joining the club, and spaces may be limited. Once you register, your QR pass is your entry reference. Keep it handy and bring it to check-in.
