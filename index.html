Do I need to know how to code?

You can start exploring with no prior experience. The beginner resources on this page are a good first step. Ask the club about the right starting point for you.

Do I need a Mac to get started?

You can explore app ideas, design, and Swift concepts before choosing your tools. Xcode runs on a Mac; Apple also offers Swift Playground on iPad and Mac.

How do I join the club?

Visit the Join the Club page for Apple sign-in availability. While online registration is being prepared, contact the club on Instagram to express your interest. Signing in alone does not confirm membership.

Core Team

Alex Chen

Club President

Alex leads the Apple Developer Club at SGU, bringing a passion for elegant design and seamless user experiences. With extensive experience in Swift and iOS development, he has built multiple apps featured on the App Store.

"The best part about this community is seeing people go from writing their first line of code to launching a full product," says Alex.

Sarah Jenkins

Vice President & Technical Lead

Sarah oversees the technical curriculum for the club, specializing in SwiftUI and Core ML. She ensures that workshops are both challenging and accessible to members of all skill levels.

Before joining SGU, Sarah was a Swift Student Challenge Distinguished Winner. She believes strongly in learning by building.

Marcus Johnson

Design Lead

Marcus brings the Human Interface Guidelines to life. As the Design Lead, he mentors students on how to make their apps not just functional, but intuitively beautiful.

"Good design is invisible," Marcus notes. "Our goal is to teach students how to bridge the gap between engineering and art."

Emily Rodriguez

Events Coordinator

Emily is the heartbeat of the community, organizing everything from weekly meetups to massive hackathons. Her energy keeps the club vibrant and connected.

She recently coordinated a successful trip for 20 students to attend a regional developer conference, expanding the club's network dramatically.
